# Challenge 1 - Bundesliga Wrapped
**AWS World Sports Innovation Cup 2026**

## 1. Background
Personalized digital experiences have transformed the way fans engage with their favorite brands and platforms. One of the most iconic examples is "Spotify Wrapped," which gives users a unique, data-driven summary of their listening habits each year, sparking excitement, sharing, and conversation across social media. 

Yet, despite the Bundesliga's rich trove of match data and user interactions, there is currently no equivalent feature that offers fans a personalized, season-long reflection of their football journey. At present, the Bundesliga app delivers engaging content through match stories and Bundesliga stories, allowing users to swipe through curated slides. However, these formats are not tailored to individual preferences, nor do they provide a wrap-up of a user's favorite moments, clubs, or players over weeks, months, or the entire season.

## 2. Challenge
This challenge asks you to design a first draft of "Bundesliga Wrapped", a personalized, shareable summary feature for the **Bundesliga app (React Native)**. 

**Key Requirements:**
* Use available data to highlight key moments, stats, and fandom for each user.
* Include a basic business plan covering delivery frequency, engagement, and marketing ideas.
* **Automation and Reusability:** Your solution must be designed so that the system automatically generates meaningful summaries for *any* club when provided with the same data structure, without manual reconfiguration.

**Provided Data:**
1. Statistics of a Bundesliga club and its players from the 2024-25 season.
2. Overview of Bundesliga players, clubs, and games (with IDs) from the 2024-25 season.
3. User mock data for preferences within the Bundesliga app.
4. Video content and player images for the respective club from the 2024-25 season.
